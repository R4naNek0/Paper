and General Growth Generators

Abstract This paper aims at solving a multidimensional backward stochastic diﬀerential equation
(BSDE) whose generator g satisﬁes a weak monotonicity condition and a general growth condition
in y. We ﬁrst establish an existence and uniqueness result of solutions for this kind of BSDEs by
using systematically the technique of the priori estimation, the convolution approach, the iteration,
the truncation and the Bihari inequality. Then, we overview some assumptions related closely to the
monotonicity condition in the literature and compare them in an eﬀective way, which yields that our
existence and uniqueness result really and truly uniﬁes the Mao condition in y and the monotonicity
condition with the general growth condition in y, and it generalizes some known results. Finally, we
prove a stability theorem and a comparison theorem for this kind of BSDEs, which also improves some
known results.
Keywords Backward stochastic diﬀerential equation, existence and uniqueness, weak monotonicity
condition, general growth condition, comparison theorem

BSDEs, in a nonlinear form, were initially introduced by Pardoux and Peng [16] who established an existence and uniqueness result of solutions under the Lipschitz assumption of the
generator g. Since then, much eﬀort has been made to weaken the Lipschitz hypothesis on g,
for instance, in the one-dimensional setting (k = 1), Lepeltier and San Martin [14] proved the
existence of a solution for BSDE (1.1) when g is continuous and of linear growth in (y, z), and
Fan et al. [7] extended it to the case where the time horizon T may be inﬁnite. Kobylanski [12]
obtained the existence and uniqueness of a solution when g has a quadratic growth in z and the
terminal condition ξ is bounded, and then [2, 3] further extended the result of [12] to the case of
unbounded terminal conditions. In the multidimensional case, Hamadène [11] and Fan et al. [8]
investigated the existence and uniqueness of a solution for (1.1) where g is uniformly continuous
in (y, z) and for each i = 1, 2, . . . , k, the i-th component gi of g depends only on the i-th row
of the matrix z. Mao [15] obtained an existence and uniqueness result of a solution for (1.1),
where g satisﬁes a particular non-Lipschitz condition in y called usually the Mao condition in
the literature, and [9, 10] extended it to the case where the time horizon T may be inﬁnite.
Peng [19] ﬁrst introduced a monotonicity condition in y for g, and under this monotonicity
condition as well as a general growth condition in y for g, Pardoux [17] established an existence
and uniqueness result of a solution for (1.1). Using the same monotonicity condition and more
general growth condition in y for g, Briand et al. [4] investigated the existence and uniqueness
of a solution in Lp (p > 1) for (1.1). Furthermore, Situ [20] put forward a weak monotonicity condition as well as a linear growth condition in y for g and considered the existence and
uniqueness of solutions to BSDEs with jumps. The reader is referred to [18, 21–24] for some

further results on BSDEs with jumps. We should point out that all of these results need the
linear growth condition in y for g.
This paper is interested in solving a multidimensional BSDE whose generator g satisﬁes the
weak monotonicity condition and the general growth condition in y mentioned above. We ﬁrst
establish an existence and uniqueness result of solutions for this kind of BSDEs (see Theorem 2.1
in Section 2), which is one of the main results of this paper. It should be mentioned that in
the procedure of the proof of Theorem 2.1, some new diﬃculties arise due to the combination
of the weak monotonicity condition and the general growth condition in y for g, particularly,
the weak convergence technique used in [17] cannot work in our setting. These diﬃculties are
all overcomed by using systematically the technique of the a priori estimation, the convolution
approach, the iteration, the truncation and the Bihari inequality. Then, we overview some
assumptions related closely to the monotonicity condition in the literature and compare them
in an eﬀective way, which yields that Theorem 2.1 really and truly uniﬁes the Mao condition in y
and the monotonicity condition with the general growth condition in y, and it generalizes some
corresponding results in [6, 11, 15–17]. Finally, we prove a stability theorem and a comparison
theorem for this kind of BSDEs, which also improves the corresponding comparison results
in [5] and [17].
This paper is organized as follows. In Section 2, we put forward the existence and uniqueness
result and introduce some remarks and examples to show that it generalizes the corresponding
results mentioned above. In Section 3, we start with a priori estimate and then prove the
existence and uniqueness result. Then, we establish a stability theorem in Section 4 and a
comparison theorem in Section 5. Finally, some technical details in Section 2, including the
proof of the relations of the assumptions related to the monotonicity condition, are provided in
Appendix.
