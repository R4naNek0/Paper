Bounded solutions for general time interval BSDEs with
quadratic growth coeﬃcients and stochastic conditions

Huan-Huan Luo and Sheng-Jun Fan∗
School of Mathematics,
China University of Mining and Technology,
Xuzhou, Jiangsu 221116, P. R. China
∗f s j@126.com
Received 11 April 2016
Revised 4 June 2017
Accepted 15 July 2017
Published 17 August 2017
This paper deals with bounded solutions for general time interval one-dimensional
backward stochastic diﬀerential equations (BSDEs for short) with quadratic growth
coeﬃcients and stochastic conditions. Several general results of existence, uniqueness,
stability and comparison for the bounded solutions are put forward and established,
which improve considerably some existing works, even though for the case of ﬁnite time
interval. Some new ideas are also developed to establish these results.
Keywords: Backward stochastic diﬀerential equation; general time interval; bounded
solution; stochastic condition; quadratic growth.
AMS Subject Classiﬁcation: 60H10

1. Introduction
Throughout this paper, let us always ﬁx a positive integer d and a positive real
number T which can be ﬁnite or inﬁnite. Let (Ω, F , P ) be a complete probability
space carrying a standard d-dimensional Brownian motion (Bt )t≥0 , (Ft )t≥0 the
completed natural σ-algebra generated by (Bt )t≥0 and FT := F . We consider the
following one-dimensional backward stochastic diﬀerential equation (BSDE):

continuous with respect to (y, z), called the generator. The triple (ξ, T, g) is called
the parameters of BSDE (1.1), and usually denoted by BSDE (ξ, g) the BSDE
whose generator is g and whose terminal condition is ξ. The solution of BSDE (1.1)
is a pair of (Ft )-progressively measurable processes (yt , zt )t∈[0,T ] .
The nonlinear version of multidimensional BSDEs was ﬁrst introduced by
Pardoux and Peng [19], which established an existence and uniqueness result of
square-integral solutions for BSDE (1.1) under the uniform Lipschitz condition of
the generator g. Since then, BSDEs have been extensively studied due to its applications in many ﬁelds such as stochastic games, optimal control, mathematical
ﬁnance and PDEs and so on, see [2, 11, 18, 20]. And many eﬀorts have been made
to improve the result of Pardoux and Peng [19] by weakening the uniform Lipschitz
condition of the generator g, relaxing the ﬁnite time terminal T to a stopping time
or inﬁnity and generalizing the terminal condition ξ to non-square integrable case,
see [1–10, 12, 13, 16, 17, 21] etc.
In particular, we would like to mention the following works related closely to
our works. First of all, in 1997 El Karoui and Huang [7] introduced, at the ﬁrst
time, a kind of stochastic Lipschitz condition of the generator g in (y, z), and established a general existence and uniqueness result for solutions of BSDE (1.1). This
kind of condition was also employed in Bender and Kohlmann [1] and Wang et al.
[21]. After that, in 2008 Briand and Confortola [2] put forward another kind of
stochastic Lipschitz condition involving a BMO-martingale and obtained an existence and uniqueness result for solutions of BSDE (1.1). However, these works need
some additional and stronger integrability assumptions with respect to the parameters (ξ, g) and the solutions. Recently, Liu et al. [17] eliminated these additional
assumptions and established several existence, uniqueness and comparison results
of Lp (p > 1) solutions for BSDEs (see Lemmas 2.1 and 2.2 for details), where the
generator g satisﬁes some new stochastic conditions. On the other hand, we have
to mention that Kobylanski [15] ﬁrst introduced the quadratic growth condition
of the generator g in z and investigated existence, uniqueness and stability of the
bounded solutions for BSDE (1.1). And, BSDE with quadratic growth generator
has attracted more and more interest in recent years due to its close connections
with PDEs and mathematical ﬁnance, see, for example, Briand and Hu [4, 5], Delbaen et al. [6], Fan [9], Hu and Tang [12], Lepeltier and San Martin [16] and Morlais
[18] etc.
Enlightened by these works, this paper is devoted to the bounded solutions of
general time interval BSDEs with quadratic growth generators and some certain
stochastic conditions. Several general results of existence, uniqueness, comparison
and stability for the bounded solutions are established, which improve considerably
some existing results, even though for the case of ﬁnite time interval. In addition, we
also point out that under our weaker assumptions, some of the ideas and techniques
employed in previous works are not valid. Consequently, we have to develop some
new ideas to solve our problems.

The remaining of this paper is organized as follows. In Sec. 2, we introduce
some basic notations, deﬁnitions and three lemmas. Section 3 aims at establishing
a general monotonic stability result of bounded solutions for BSDEs (see Proposition 3.1), which is also a powerful tool to prove the existence of the solutions and
generalizes the corresponding part of Theorem 2 in Lepeltier and San Martin [16] as
well as Proposition 2.4 in Kobylanski [15] (see Remark 3.1). And, Sec. 4 is devoted
to the uniqueness of the bounded solutions by establishing a general comparison
theorem for BSDEs with quadratic growth generators and stochastic conditions (see
Theorem 4.1), which improves considerably the corresponding comparison results
obtained in Briand and Hu [5] and Fan [9] (see Remark 4.3). Based on Proposition 3.1 and Theorem 4.1 together with the techniques of truncation, convolution,
exponential transform and weak convergence, in Sec. 5 we put forward and prove
a general existence result of the bounded solutions for BSDEs (see Theorem 5.1),
where the generator g has a stochastic semi-linear growth in y and a quadratic
growth in z. This generalizes, to some extent, some corresponding existence results
for the bounded solutions of BSDEs with quadratic growth coeﬃcients obtained
respectively in Lepeltier and San Martin [16], Kobylanski [15], Briand and Hu [4, 5]
and Fan [9]. Here, we specially mention that the usual PDE-based or ODE-based
ideas employed in Lepeltier and San Martin [16], Kobylanski [15] and Fan [9] are
not eﬀective anymore in our stochastic framework. Finally, in Sec. 6 we establish
a general existence result of the minimal (resp. maximal) bounded solution for
BSDEs with certain quadratic growth generators and stochastic conditions (see
Theorem 6.1), which generalizes partly Theorem 3.1 in Fan [9] and Theorem 2 in
Lepeltier and San Martin [16]. Several examples are also provided in Secs. 5 and 6
to illustrate that our results improve some existing works.

4. Comparison Theorem of Bounded Solutions
In this section, we aim at establishing a general comparison theorem of bounded
solutions which will be used several times later. We will use the following assumptions on the generator g, where 0 < T ≤ +∞.
(H1) There exists a stochastic process u· ∈ L∞ (Ω; L1 ([0, T ]; R+)) such that dP ×

sgn(y1 − y2 )(g(ω, t, y1 , z) − g(ω, t, y2 , z)) ≤ ut (ω)|y1 − y2 |.
(H2) There exist a continuous function h(·) : R → R+ , a stochastic process v· ∈
