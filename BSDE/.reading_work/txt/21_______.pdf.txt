Abstract
In section 1 of this paper, some important results on backward stochastic differential equations introduced by Peng shege (BSDEs in short), which will be usefull
in the remaining of this paper, are introduced, under this fundamental framework,
three important questions on BSDEs are respectively investigated in the following
sections developing and enriching the theory of BSDEs, they are listed as follows:
in section 2, under some proper conditions, some convergent theorems of the gevaluation operator are obtained; in sections 3, under the most elementary conditions on BSDEs, a general relationship between the generator g of BSDEs and
the conditional g-evaluation system is put forward and proved, furthermore, by this
general relationship, some specific relationships between them are investigated and
some converse comparison theorems for BSDEs and some uniquence theorems for
generators of BSDEs are obtained; in section 4, samely under the most elementary
conditions on BSDEs, the sufficient and neccessary conditions under which Jensen’s
inequality of one element function and bivariate function for g-evaluation holds in
general are put forward and proved. It is worthwhile to mention that some further
